Delete Your A Game Account & Data

You can permanently delete your A Game account and associated personal data at any time. Apple and Google require apps that let users create an account to also offer account deletion — here is how.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open A Game.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account (Account settings).
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are scheduled for deletion immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@a-game.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account." We'll verify it's you and process the deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials.
  • Your profile and onboarding details (age, gender, preferences, goals).
  • Content you submitted: uploaded screenshots and images, pasted conversations, personas, roleplay messages,

and challenge proof.

  • Your usage history, A-Game Score, and score history.

What we may retain

We may keep limited records where the law requires it (for example, transaction/tax records) or to prevent fraud and abuse. Any retained data is minimized and protected under our Privacy Policy.

Timing

Deletion from active systems happens within a commercially reasonable period after your request. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on a rolling schedule.

A note on subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it separately in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings (see Support).

Questions? Email support@a-game.com.
