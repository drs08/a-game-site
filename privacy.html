Privacy Policy — A Game

Effective date: September 8, 2026 Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A Game ("A Game", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Mapaan AI, collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the A Game mobile application and related services (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

A Game is an AI-powered dating-coaching assistant. It helps adults improve their dating conversations, profiles, and confidence through features such as Chat Analysis, Profile Review, Opener suggestions, AI Roleplay Practice, a Daily Challenge, and an A-Game Score.

1. Who this policy is for

The App is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us at the email in Section 12.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information. Your email address and password (passwords are stored only as salted hashes by

our authentication provider; we never see your plaintext password). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and the email you authorize that provider to share.

  • Onboarding and profile details. Information you choose to enter to personalize coaching, such as your

age range, gender, who you are interested in dating, your dating goal, communication style, and vibe preferences.

  • Content you submit for analysis. Screenshots and images of dating-app conversations and profiles,

pasted conversation text, persona details you create for roleplay practice, messages you type during roleplay, and any proof images you upload for the Daily Challenge.

  • Support communications. Anything you send us when you contact support.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data. Which features you use and how often (for example, counts used against your plan limits),

session activity, your A-Game Score and score history, and in-app events needed to run the App.

  • Device and technical data. Basic device and app information such as app version, operating system,

and coarse diagnostic/crash information used to keep the App working.

2.3 Payment information

If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play and, for entitlement management, by RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full card number or bank details. We receive confirmation of your subscription status (for example, active weekly or monthly plan, renewal, or cancellation) so we can unlock features.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the App's core features (analyze chats and profiles, generate openers, run roleplay practice,

score your progress, and deliver daily challenges).

  • Personalize coaching based on the preferences you provide (gender, who you date, goals, style).
  • Enforce plan limits and manage your subscription and entitlements.
  • Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use the content you submit (your screenshots, chats, or roleplay messages) to train our own AI models, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. AI processing of your content

Core features work by sending your submitted content (screenshots, pasted text, roleplay messages, persona details) to third-party AI model providers that generate the coaching output. Today those providers are:

  • Anthropic (Claude models)
  • Google (Gemini models)

These providers process your content solely to return a result to the App. Under our arrangements with these providers, submitted content is not used to train their models. Their handling of data is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit content you are not comfortable sharing for this purpose, and avoid including other people's sensitive personal information where you can (for example, mask names, faces, or contact details in screenshots when it is not needed for the analysis).

5. How we share information

We share information only with:

  • Service providers ("sub-processors") who run the App on our behalf, under contracts that limit their

use of the data:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, and backend functions.
  • Anthropic and Google — AI model processing (see Section 4).
  • RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play — subscription and purchase processing.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety,

and security of our users, the public, or A Game.

  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be

transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App.
  • You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile settings) or by emailing us

(see Section 12 and the separate Account Deletion page). When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a commercially reasonable period, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.

  • Backups containing your data are purged on a rolling schedule.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your account by using a strong, unique password and keeping your device secure.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise many of these directly in the App (edit your preferences, delete your account) or by contacting us at the email in Section 12. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

EU/UK (GDPR): Our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract (to provide the App), your consent (for optional personalization), our legitimate interests (to secure and improve the App), and legal obligations. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of sensitive personal information, and the right not to be sold or to have your data "shared" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell or share your information in that sense.

India (DPDP Act, 2023): As a data fiduciary based in India, we process your personal data for the purposes described here. You have the right to access, correct, and erase your data, to grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights. To make a request or raise a grievance, contact us at the email in Section 12.

9. International transfers

We and our service providers may process your information in countries other than where you live, including the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

10. Third-party links and services

The App relies on third-party platforms (Apple, Google, Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat). Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the practices of services we do not control.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
